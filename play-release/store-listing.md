## Full description (max 4000 chars)
መዝሙረ ዳዊት — Mezmure Dawit

The Psalms of David, in its traditional trilingual format for the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo prayer tradition:

• Ge'ez (ግዕዝ)
• Amharic (አማርኛ)
• English — KJV

150 psalms organized for daily recitation, following the weekly prayer schedule of the Ethiopian Orthodox Church:

• Morning, Midday and Evening prayer collections
• Today's psalms based on the liturgical day of the week
• Full search and filters by day and prayer time
• Favorite psalms for quick access
• Adjustable text size for comfortable reading
• Dark and light themes
• Works completely offline — no account, no sign-in, no network required

Whether you pray through the psalms at dawn, at noon, or at dusk, Mezmure Dawit is a quiet, ad-free reading experience for the Amharic/Ge'ez-speaking faithful and anyone drawn to the Orthodox tradition.

Psalms of David. In three languages. Wherever you are.
