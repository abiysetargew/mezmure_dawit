Mezmure Dawit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Mezmure Dawit (“the app”) is a religious reading app displaying the psalms of David in Ge’ez, Amharic and English. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

What the app collects

The app itself does nothing with your data:

  • No account is required or possible.
  • No personal information is collected, transmitted, sold or shared by the developer.
  • Favorites, reading preferences, theme and font size are stored only on your device (SharedPreferences) and never leave it.
  • No location access, no camera, no contacts, no microphone, no files.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob uses the advertising ID to serve and measure ads. Data collected by Google via AdMob is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings (Settings → Google → Ads), and opt out of ad personalization at google.com/settings/ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be posted on the store listing.
